APPLY ONE EXERCISE TO ANY PROMPT

Three exercises for clearer speaking

01 / FOCUS

Make one point

Start with a direct answer in one sentence. Give one reason and a concrete example, then return to your point. Use this structure for impromptu speaking and questions in meetings.

02 / PRECISION

Replace a vague phrase

Replace “We need to communicate better” with “Let's send one update every Friday with the decision, owner, and next step.” Find one phrase in your answer to make more specific.

03 / STRUCTURE

Deliver a concise update

Give the status, a relevant detail, and the next action: “The draft is ready. We still need the revised figures. Please send them by Thursday so we can review on Friday.”

What is articulation training?

In this library, articulation training means organizing and expressing ideas clearly. The exercises focus on communication for adults: making a point, choosing precise words, and helping a listener follow. This is speaking practice, rather than clinical speech therapy or pronunciation assessment.

How can I practice public speaking on my own?

Choose a prompt, plan one main point, and answer aloud for 30 seconds to two minutes. Check whether your point, example, and ending were clear. Repeat the answer with one focused change. For a longer routine, use the 30-day articulation training plan for adults.

Which exercises fit professional communication?

Use meeting prompts to practice updates and recommendations, interview prompts to explain your experience, and impromptu prompts to answer unexpected questions. The professional articulation guide covers meetings, presentations, and workplace conversations in more detail.

Is this the Articulated AI speech coach?

This is Articulated's free speaking practice library. The prompts, timer, and self-review exercises work without an account.
